How this is measured

These figures come from UptimeRobot, an independent monitoring service. It requests our public addresses over the internet every 5 minutes, from outside our servers, the way a visitor would, and we only read its record. We do not edit it. A check that fails or times out counts as downtime, so outages shorter than 5 minutes may not show. Days are UTC.{% if status_page_url %} You can see the same data on UptimeRobot's own status page, which stays up even if our servers do not.{% endif %}
