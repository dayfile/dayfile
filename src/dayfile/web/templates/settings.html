Download your log

Download everything in your log as a SQLite database (gzipped). You can open it with any SQLite tool.

Start a fresh log

A log has no way to delete single events, so when it fills up (see Usage above) the way out is a new, empty one under the same name. This erases every event, token and feed in it. Anything that posts or reads with an old token stops working until you make new ones. Download your log first.
