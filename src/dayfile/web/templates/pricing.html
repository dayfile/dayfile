Pricing

dayfile is free while it is in beta.

Placeholder. Paid plans are not settled yet. Nothing on this page is an offer, and the numbers below, which are also listed under Limits in the docs, can change before the beta ends.

Beta plan: free

  • Your own log at <username>.dayfile.cloud, with the web app, feeds and tokens.
  • Up to 50,000 events and 25 MB of storage per log. When a log is full, new events are refused until you start a fresh log (Settings) or ask us for more room.
  • Up to 60 writes a minute per write token and 300 reads a minute per read token.
  • Atom, JSON Feed and RSS feeds, and a download of your whole log as a SQLite file whenever you like.

After the beta

We plan a free tier for personal use and a paid plan with higher limits. We will announce prices here and by email to people with a log, well before anything changes. Nothing you stored will be deleted for going over a future limit without notice, and you can export your whole log at any time from Settings.

dayfile is open source (AGPL-3.0), so you can also run it yourself for nothing.

Questions? Write to team@dayfile.app.
