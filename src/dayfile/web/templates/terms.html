7. Deletion

When you delete your account (in Settings) or ask us to delete a log, it stops working and we erase the live copy at once. Our encrypted backups, which are kept for 14 days, age out within 14 days. Events cannot be removed one at a time, because the log is append-only. Deleting an account is final: we cannot restore it. You can sign up again with the same GitHub account as a new, empty account.
